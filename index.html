CapyBot · Root Community Bot

Privacy Policy

This policy describes how CapyBot processes and retains information while providing community moderation and management features.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Introduction

CapyBot is a community management and moderation bot designed for the Root platform. It provides features intended to support community administration, including automated moderation, warning records, welcome messages, role management, announcements, and authorized voice-channel operations.

This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information CapyBot may process, the purposes for which that information is used, how it may be stored, and the circumstances in which it may be accessed by others.

The information processed depends on the features enabled, the commands used, and the configuration established by community administrators.

2. Information Processed

CapyBot processes information made available through Root and information generated by its operation when necessary to provide its configured features.

2.1. Member and Community Information

This may include:

  • Member and community identifiers.
  • Available display names or nicknames.
  • Channel and role identifiers.
  • Role assignments and relevant reaction events.
  • Community configuration and moderation settings.

This information is used to identify members, apply configured permissions, manage role assignments, and perform community management operations.

2.2. Messages and Moderation Events

When automated moderation or command processing is active, CapyBot may inspect message content and associated metadata to identify configured rule violations. Depending on the enabled features, this may include:

  • Configured Arabic profanity, insults, and abusive language.
  • Repeated messages and suspected spam.
  • Excessive mentions and prohibited external links.
  • Commands submitted by members and moderators.

When a message triggers a moderation rule, CapyBot may retain a limited content-derived fingerprint to prevent duplicate processing. The bot may also record the associated message ID, channel ID, member ID, detected rule, and moderation outcome.

CapyBot is not designed to maintain a permanent archive of all community messages as part of its standard moderation workflow. This does not prevent Root or other services from retaining their own message records.

2.3. Warnings and Moderation History

When a warning or other supported moderation action is recorded, CapyBot may store:

  • The affected member's identifier.
  • The moderator's identifier or an automated-action designation.
  • The reason and type of moderation action.
  • The date and time the record was created.
  • Related message and channel identifiers, where available.
  • Whether the warning remains active.

These records support moderation reviews, help administrators identify repeated violations, and provide a history of recorded incidents.

2.4. Role Management

When reaction roles are configured, CapyBot processes relevant message and reaction identifiers, emoji identifiers, member identifiers, and role identifiers. It retains the mappings needed to assign or remove roles according to the configured rules.

2.5. Voice-Channel Activity

Voice management features may process member identifiers, voice channel identifiers, and participation events to support authorized actions such as listing participants, muting, deafening, or removing a member from a voice channel.

CapyBot's standard voice management features do not require the recording or storage of microphone audio or private voice conversations.

2.6. Configuration and Integrations

CapyBot stores configuration information required to operate, including welcome and departure settings, moderation options, logging preferences, custom moderation terms, reaction-role mappings, and configured webhook addresses.

When an external webhook is used, CapyBot may send the message or notification content specified by an authorized user to the configured destination. The information received by that external service is subject to its own practices.

3. Purposes of Processing

CapyBot uses processed information to:

  • Provide member welcome and departure messages.
  • Detect configured moderation violations and suspected spam.
  • Record and display moderation warnings and infractions.
  • Support authorized role and voice-channel operations.
  • Enforce moderator permissions for administrative commands.
  • Send announcements and configured webhook notifications.
  • Maintain community configuration across restarts.
  • Investigate errors and maintain the reliability of its features.
  • Help prevent unauthorized use of administrative functions.

CapyBot does not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

4. Automated Moderation

CapyBot may automatically identify messages that match configured moderation rules. Depending on the community's settings and the permissions granted to the bot, an identified violation may result in a warning, a moderation record, a notification, or an attempt to delete the message.

Automated language detection is not infallible. Dialects, slang, context, spelling variations, and ambiguous expressions may affect the accuracy of a result. Some violations may also go undetected.

Community administrators remain responsible for reviewing moderation outcomes and determining whether further action is appropriate. Members who dispute a moderation decision should raise the matter with the relevant community administration.

5. Data Storage and Retention

CapyBot uses the storage made available to it by its operating environment to retain information needed for its functions. Persistent records may include community settings, moderation histories, custom terms, and role mappings.

Information is retained according to the requirements of the relevant feature and the storage mechanisms in use. Certain moderation records are maintained as persistent records, while temporary event-deduplication data is intended to expire after a defined period.

  • Configuration data: retained while needed to operate the configured features.
  • Moderation records: retained in the bot's persistent storage for administrative review, subject to the available record-management controls.
  • Temporary event data: intended to be retained only for the relevant processing period.

Authorized administrators may use available bot controls to remove warning records or change configuration. Such changes do not necessarily remove copies held independently by Root, hosting providers, or external services. Backups and system records may also remain for a limited period under the policies of the relevant provider.

6. Access and Disclosure

CapyBot does not sell personal information or disclose it to third parties for their independent advertising purposes. Information may be accessible or transmitted where necessary to operate the bot and provide its enabled functions.

This may include:

  • Root: the platform through which community information and bot operations are made available.
  • Authorized administrators and moderators: individuals permitted to access relevant moderation records, configuration, or member-management functions.
  • Infrastructure providers: services responsible for hosting, storage, or operation of the bot, where applicable.
  • Configured external services: destinations that receive information intentionally sent through a webhook or other enabled integration.

External services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. CapyBot cannot control the handling of information after it has been transmitted to an independent service.

7. Security

CapyBot is designed to restrict administrative operations to authorized users and to avoid unnecessary disclosure of sensitive information. Security also depends on the permissions granted, the hosting environment, and the way administrators configure and operate the bot.

Developer tokens, authentication credentials, webhook addresses, and other secrets should be kept confidential. Access to administrative features should be limited to trusted members and roles, and bot permissions should be restricted to those required for its intended functions.

No software or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Unauthorized access, system failures, or vulnerabilities may occur despite reasonable precautions.

8. Privacy Requests

Requests concerning moderation records, inaccurate information, or the removal of information should be directed to the relevant community administration. Requests may be assessed in light of the available records, the bot's capabilities, legitimate moderation requirements, platform rules, and applicable law.

Information held independently by Root or another service may need to be addressed through that service's own procedures. Removing information from CapyBot does not automatically remove copies held elsewhere.

9. Children's Privacy

CapyBot is a community management tool and is not designed to collect additional personal information directly from children. It may process identifiers and other information made available through Root when necessary to provide the community features enabled by its administrators.

Community administrators are responsible for ensuring that their communities comply with applicable age restrictions, platform requirements, and relevant privacy laws.

10. International Processing

Depending on the location of Root's infrastructure, the bot's hosting environment, and any configured external services, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country in which a member resides. The safeguards applicable to that processing depend on the services and infrastructure used.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised when CapyBot's features, information-handling practices, or applicable requirements change. The date displayed at the beginning of this document identifies the most recent revision.

Continued operation of CapyBot following publication of a revised policy is subject to the applicable platform rules and legal requirements.
